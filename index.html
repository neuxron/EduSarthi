About EduSarthi

Bringing Education To Rural India

EduSarthi is specially designed for students living in rural and remote areas where access to quality education and stable internet connectivity is limited.

Our mission is to ensure that every student gets access to proper learning resources, syllabus guidance, notes and academic support regardless of their location.

Our Mission

Making Education Accessible & Affordable

We aim to help students continue their studies without depending heavily on expensive coaching institutes or high-speed internet services.

📘 Providing syllabus-based learning resources
📄 Offering downloadable notes for offline study
🎯 Helping students prepare for school exams effectively
🌱 Encouraging self-learning in villages and remote regions
